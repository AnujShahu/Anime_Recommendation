⚙️ How It Works

1. Dual-Tier Indexing: We maintain a high-speed local database of 7,400+ curated titles for instant sub-millisecond search and genre filtering.

2. Live API Fallback: If a searched title is brand new or airing this current season, our backend queries the AniList GraphQL engine (with secondary Jikan fallback) to deliver up-to-the-minute poster art, scores, and airing status.

3. Content Matching: Matches anime based on intersecting thematic genres, rankings, and popularity metrics.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do recommendations work?

When you search for a title (e.g. Hunter x Hunter), the engine identifies its primary genre signatures (such as Action, Adventure, Fantasy) and matches related series sharing similar themes, scoring, and popularity.

What is the difference between Watchlist and Favorites?

Watchlist: Use this to queue up anime you plan to watch or are currently watching.
Favorites: Use this for your all-time favorite masterworks that define your personal taste!

Why do some anime have scores and others say "N/A"?

Scores are compiled from MyAnimeList and AniList community ratings. Brand-new or upcoming series that haven't aired yet may not have received enough ratings to establish a global score.

Can I change my preferred genres after onboarding?

Yes! Navigate to your Profile Settings at any time to update your genre preferences.

Where does the data and imagery come from?

Posters, descriptions, and scores are provided via public open-source APIs from AniList and MyAnimeList (via Jikan). All media rights belong to their respective studios and licensors. Read our full Copyright Disclaimer.
